# Employee Handbook — Store Operations

**Document type:** Handbook · **Audience:** all store associates and shift leads

## Shift structure and duties

The store runs three shifts: opening, mid, and closing. Every associate is
assigned a home aisle from the eight sales aisles (Produce, Dairy, Bakery,
Frozen, Beverages, Snacks, Household, Personal Care) but is expected to help
across the floor during peaks.

**Opening shift**

- Walk the assigned aisle and record any overnight gaps or spoilage.
- Face and front all stock before doors open.
- Complete the assigned cycle counts (see below).
- Confirm price-change and promotion tags match the current plan.

**Mid shift**

- Work the delivery: receive, date-stamp perishables, stage by aisle, and
  replenish stockouts first.
- Re-face high-velocity end-caps after the lunch peak.
- Pull any perishable stock within two days of expiry to markdown.

**Closing shift**

- Final face and front.
- Sweep for misplaced and concealed stock (a loss-prevention check).
- Record closing gaps so the opening shift can prioritise.

## Stock count cadence

Cycle counting is continuous, not a single annual event:

- **High-velocity SKUs** (fast movers, typically Beverages, Snacks, Dairy):
  counted weekly.
- **Mid-velocity SKUs:** counted every two weeks.
- **Slow movers** (much of Household and Personal Care): counted monthly.
- **Full aisle counts:** one aisle per week on rotation, so every aisle is
  fully counted at least quarterly.

Counts are entered into the stockroom system the same shift. A count that
differs from the recorded on-hand figure by more than the tolerance in the
Inventory Count and Discrepancy SOP is logged as a discrepancy and escalated.

## Incident reporting

Any of the following is an incident and must be logged the same shift:

- suspected theft or a confrontation with a customer or third party;
- stock found concealed, opened, or consumed on the premises;
- a delivery arriving damaged, short, or late;
- equipment failure affecting the cold chain (freezer or chiller);
- an injury or a spill.

Incidents are recorded with time, location, people involved, and immediate
action taken. Cold-chain failures also trigger a quality assessment of the
affected Frozen or Dairy stock before it goes back on sale.

## Escalation paths

1. **Associate → Shift Lead** for anything outside routine: a stockout on a
   fast mover, a count discrepancy, a customer dispute, a damaged delivery.
2. **Shift Lead → Store Manager** for discrepancies above tolerance, refund
   overrides above the register limit, repeat stockouts, and all suspected
   theft.
3. **Store Manager → Inventory and Supply Chain Manager** for supplier
   performance problems: repeated late deliveries, short shipments, or
   lead-time breaches against the supplier contract.
4. **Store Manager → Loss Prevention** for shrinkage that crosses the
   investigation threshold in the Shrinkage and Loss Prevention SOP.

## Working with the operations assistant

The operations assistant answers questions about sales, stock, margins,
suppliers and policy using live store data and these documents. Treat its
root-cause reports as a starting point for investigation: confirm the physical
facts on the floor before acting on a recommendation, and record what you
found.
