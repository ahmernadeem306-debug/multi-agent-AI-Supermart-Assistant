# Supplier Agreement — FreshFarm Produce Co.

**Document type:** Contract · **Supplier reference:** vendor account "FreshFarm" · **Categories supplied:** Produce, and selected Bakery lines

## 1. Scope

FreshFarm Produce Co. supplies fresh fruit and vegetables for the Produce
aisle and par-baked lines for the Bakery aisle. This agreement sets lead
times, delivery performance standards, order minimums, pricing and credit
terms. It is the reference used when the operations assistant evaluates a
Produce stockout or a supply delay.

## 2. Lead times and ordering

- **Standard lead time:** 3 calendar days from purchase-order submission to
  delivery at the store dock.
- **Order cut-off:** 14:00 local time. Orders placed after cut-off are treated
  as next-day orders.
- **Delivery days:** Monday, Wednesday, Friday, Saturday.
- Because Produce shelf life is short (about 7 days) and Bakery about 5 days,
  reorder points for FreshFarm SKUs are set to roughly five days of average
  demand and safety stock to roughly two days, so a single missed delivery
  does not cause a stockout.

## 3. Minimum order quantities

- Minimum order value per delivery: 250.00.
- Minimum per line: one full case. Case sizes are listed in the current price
  file.
- Orders below the minimum are held and consolidated with the next order,
  which effectively extends the lead time — buyers must plan around this.

## 4. Delivery performance and late-delivery penalties

- **On-time definition:** delivered in full on or before the promised date.
- **Target on-time-in-full rate:** 97% measured monthly.
- A delivery received **1–3 days late** incurs a credit of 5% of the affected
  lines' value.
- A delivery **4–7 days late** incurs a credit of 12% and requires a written
  explanation from FreshFarm.
- A delivery **more than 7 days late**, or a no-show, incurs a credit of 20%,
  and BizAgent may source the affected lines elsewhere for that cycle at
  FreshFarm's cost.
- Three or more late deliveries in a rolling 60-day window triggers a
  performance review and may reduce FreshFarm's allocation.

## 5. Quality and rejection

Produce is inspected on arrival. Stock that fails the freshness check is
rejected at the dock and credited in full. Product that passes inspection but
spoils before its expected shelf life, when stored correctly, is credited on
presentation of the batch code and photographs.

## 6. Pricing and credit terms

- Prices are fixed for a 13-week period and listed in the price file.
- Payment terms: net 30 days from invoice date.
- Delivery-performance credits and quality credits are applied against the
  next invoice, not paid separately.
- Either party may terminate with 60 days' written notice; BizAgent may
  terminate immediately for repeated critical late deliveries as defined in
  section 4.

## 7. Contacts

Orders and delivery queries go to the FreshFarm account desk. Escalations
about repeated lateness or lead-time breaches are handled by the BizAgent
Inventory and Supply Chain Manager.
