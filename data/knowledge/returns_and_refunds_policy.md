# Returns and Refunds Policy

**Document type:** Policy · **Owner:** Customer Service Manager · **Applies to:** all staffed registers and the service desk

## Principle

BizAgent Supermart accepts returns of merchandise that is unused, resaleable
where applicable, and within the time limits below. Staff resolve
straightforward returns at the register; anything outside policy goes to a
shift manager.

## Time limits by category

| Category / aisle | Return window | Condition required |
|---|---|---|
| Household, Personal Care | 30 days | Unopened, with receipt |
| Snacks, Beverages (non-perishable) | 30 days | Sealed, with receipt |
| Frozen | 7 days | Kept frozen, packaging intact |
| Dairy | 3 days | Within printed use-by date |
| Bakery | 2 days | Same-day or next-day only |
| Produce | 2 days | Quality complaint, any condition |

Perishable categories (Produce, Bakery, Dairy, Frozen) are refunded primarily
on **quality grounds** — spoilage, damage, or failure to meet freshness
expectations. A perishable item past its printed expiry date at the time of
purchase is always refunded in full and logged as an expiry-reason shrinkage
event, not as a customer return.

## Receipt requirements

- **With receipt:** refund to the original tender.
- **Without receipt, item under 15.00:** store credit at the current shelf
  price, once per customer per day.
- **Without receipt, item 15.00 or over:** shift manager approval required;
  refund issued as store credit.

Loyalty-account purchases can be looked up at the service desk and count as
proof of purchase.

## Manager overrides

A shift manager may override the standard window or condition when:

- the product has a genuine quality or safety defect;
- a recall is in effect;
- the customer was given incorrect information by staff; or
- the goodwill value of retaining the customer clearly exceeds the item cost.

Every override is recorded with the manager's ID, the reason code, and the
value. Overrides above 75.00 also require a note to the Store Manager.

## Non-returnable items

Gift cards, clearance items marked "final sale", and items sold visibly
damaged at a discount cannot be returned. Opened Personal Care items are
non-returnable for hygiene reasons unless defective.

## Handling returned stock

1. Inspect the item. Resaleable non-perishables are returned to the planogram
   location following FIFO.
2. Perishables, opened items, and anything of uncertain condition are written
   off. Record the write-off with reason **damage** or **expiry** as
   appropriate so loss reporting stays accurate.
3. Suspected fraud (repeated no-receipt returns, resale of stolen goods) is not
   challenged at the register; note the loyalty ID or description and refer to
   Loss Prevention under the Shrinkage and Loss Prevention SOP.

## Reporting

Return volume and refund value are reviewed weekly by category. A spike in
returns for one SKU is treated as a potential quality or supplier problem and
is raised with the Inventory and Supply Chain Manager, who checks it against
recent delivery batches and supplier performance.
