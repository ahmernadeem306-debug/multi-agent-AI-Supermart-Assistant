# Supplier Agreement — DailyGoods Distribution Ltd.

**Document type:** Contract · **Supplier reference:** vendor account "DailyGoods" · **Categories supplied:** Beverages, Snacks, Household, Personal Care, and ambient Dairy

## 1. Scope

DailyGoods Distribution Ltd. supplies ambient and packaged grocery for the
Beverages, Snacks, Household and Personal Care aisles, plus long-life Dairy
lines. Terms differ from the FreshFarm produce agreement because these
categories have long shelf lives and are ordered in larger, less frequent
drops. This document is the reference for evaluating supply delays on those
aisles.

## 2. Lead times and ordering

- **Standard lead time:** 7 calendar days from purchase-order submission to
  delivery.
- **Order cut-off:** 17:00 local time; later orders are next-day.
- **Delivery days:** Tuesday and Thursday.
- Reorder points for DailyGoods SKUs are set to roughly five days of average
  demand plus the 7-day lead time buffer, with safety stock at roughly two
  days. Slow-moving Household and Personal Care lines carry a larger buffer
  because they are counted only monthly.

## 3. Minimum order quantities

- Minimum order value per delivery: 1,000.00.
- Full-case ordering only; pallet quantities for the top 20 SKUs by volume.
- Below-minimum orders are consolidated to the next delivery day, extending
  effective lead time to as much as 10 days — buyers must not let fast movers
  approach safety stock before ordering.

## 4. Delivery performance and late-delivery penalties

- **On-time definition:** delivered in full on or before the promised date.
- **Target on-time-in-full rate:** 95% measured monthly.
- A delivery **1–5 days late** incurs a credit of 3% of the affected lines'
  value.
- A delivery **6–10 days late** incurs a credit of 8%.
- A delivery **more than 10 days late**, or a no-show, incurs a credit of 15%
  and BizAgent may buy out the shortfall at DailyGoods' cost.
- Persistent lateness (four or more late deliveries in a rolling 90-day
  window) triggers a joint performance review.

Note that DailyGoods' penalty bands are more lenient and its lead time longer
than FreshFarm's, so the same number of days late is treated as less severe
here — but a long-life SKU that still stocks out despite a 7-day lead time and
a five-day reorder buffer indicates the reorder point, not just the supplier,
needs review.

## 5. Quality and returns

Damaged cases are recorded at the dock and credited. Because shelf lives are
long, spoilage claims are rare; the main loss driver on these aisles is
in-store shrinkage rather than supplier quality.

## 6. Pricing and credit terms

- Prices reviewed quarterly with 30 days' notice of change.
- Payment terms: net 45 days from invoice date.
- Performance credits applied to the next statement.
- Termination: 90 days' written notice by either party.

## 7. Contacts

Routine ordering is automated from the replenishment worklist. Lead-time
breaches and repeated lateness are escalated by the Inventory and Supply Chain
Manager, who compares DailyGoods' recent on-time rate against the FreshFarm
account when deciding where to place substitute orders.
